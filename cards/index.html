Frequently Asked Questions

Does AI Cards upload my card photos or data anywhere?
No. Scanning, OCR, and AI field suggestions all run entirely on your device using Apple's on-device frameworks. Nothing is uploaded for recognition.
What does iCloud Sync do?
It's optional and off by default. When you turn it on, your cards (including photos) are stored in your own iCloud account so they show up on your other devices signed in with the same Apple ID. Nothing is shared with us or with anyone else.
Does AI Cards add people to my Contacts automatically?
No. Contacts are written only after you review the scanned fields and tap to confirm.
Why is a feature (like "Ask About Your Cards") missing on my device?
AI features that need Apple Intelligence only appear when your device supports it and the on-device model is ready. Go to Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri to check availability and download status. The app remains fully usable without it — OCR and rule-based field extraction still work.
Which languages are supported?
Card scanning and field extraction support Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, and English business cards.
