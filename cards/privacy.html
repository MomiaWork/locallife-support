AI Cards — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

AI Cards is designed to keep your data on your device. This policy explains exactly what the app does and does not do with your information.

What we collect

Nothing. AI Cards and its developer do not operate any server, do not receive your card photos, extracted text, or any other content from the app, and do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs of any kind.

How your data is processed

  • Card scanning: Photos you take or choose are processed entirely on your device using Apple's Vision framework for text recognition.
  • AI field suggestions: Where available, Apple Intelligence (the Foundation Models framework) runs entirely on your device to suggest fields, drafts, and answers. No card data is sent off your device for this processing.
  • Storage: Your cards, photos, and notes are stored in a local database on your device.

Optional iCloud Sync

AI Cards includes an optional iCloud Sync feature, off by default. If you turn it on, your cards (including photos) are stored in your own private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit, so they can appear on your other devices signed in with the same Apple ID. This data is stored in a private database that only you can access — the developer of AI Cards has no access to it, and it is never shared with any third party.

Contacts and Reminders

AI Cards only requests access to Contacts or Reminders when you explicitly tap an action ("Save to Contacts" or "Set Reminder") after reviewing the suggested information on screen. Nothing is written to your Contacts or Reminders automatically.

Children's Privacy

AI Cards is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from children, because it does not collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised "Last updated" date.

AI名片簿——隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 9 月 24 日

AI名片簿的設計原則是把你的資料留在裝置上。這份政策誠實說明這個 App 會、以及不會對你的資料做什麼。

我們收集什麼資料

什麼都不收集。 AI名片簿跟開發者本身沒有任何伺服器，不會接收你的名片照片、辨識出的文字，或任何其他 App 內容，也沒有使用任何分析、廣告或追蹤 SDK。

你的資料如何被處理

  • 名片掃描：你拍攝或選取的照片，全部在你的裝置上用 Apple 的 Vision 框架完成文字辨識，不會離開裝置。
  • AI 欄位建議：在裝置支援的情況下，Apple Intelligence（Foundation Models 框架）全程在裝置上運作，建議欄位、草擬文字、回答問題。這個過程中沒有任何名片資料被送出裝置。
  • 儲存：你的名片、照片與備註都存在裝置上的本機資料庫。

可選的 iCloud 同步

AI名片簿有一個可選的 iCloud 同步功能，預設關閉。如果你打開它，你的名片（含照片）會透過 Apple 的 CloudKit 存進你自己的私人 iCloud 帳號，讓同一個 Apple ID 登入的其他裝置也能看到。這份資料存在只有你能存取的私有資料庫裡——AI名片簿的開發者無法存取，也不會分享給任何第三方。

通訊錄與提醒事項

AI名片簿只有在你明確點擊「儲存到通訊錄」或「設定提醒」、並且已經看過畫面上的建議內容之後，才會請求存取通訊錄或提醒事項的權限。不會有任何內容被自動寫入。

兒童隱私

AI名片簿不是針對兒童設計的 App，也不會在明知情況下收集任何兒童的資料——因為它本來就不收集任何人的資料。

政策異動

如果這份政策有異動，更新版本會用同一個網址發布，並更新上方的「最後更新」日期。
