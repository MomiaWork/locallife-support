Frequently Asked Questions

Does LocalLife Expenses upload my receipts anywhere?
No. Scanning and AI-drafted expense entries run entirely on your device. Nothing is uploaded for recognition.
Why is an AI feature missing on my device?
AI features that need Apple Intelligence only appear when your device supports it and the on-device model is ready. Go to Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri to check availability. The app remains fully usable without it.
