AI Notes — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

AI Notes is designed to keep your data on your device. This policy explains exactly what the app does and does not do with your information.

What we collect

Nothing. AI Notes and its developer do not operate any server, do not require an account, do not receive your notes, to-dos, photos or any other content from the app, and do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs of any kind.

How your data is processed

  • On-device AI: Finding to-dos and dates, reading text in photos, and turning speech into text all run on your device using Apple Intelligence (the Foundation Models framework), Vision, and on-device speech recognition. Nothing is uploaded for this processing.
  • Storage: Your notes, to-dos, photos, and settings are stored in a local database on your device.

Optional iCloud Sync

AI Notes includes an optional iCloud Sync feature, off by default. If you turn it on, your notes and to-dos (including attached photos) are stored in your own private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit, so they can appear on your other devices signed in with the same Apple ID. The developer of AI Notes has no access to this data, and it is never shared with any third party.

Permissions

  • Camera and Photos: to take or choose photos of posters, notices, bills, and warranties and find events and dates on your device.
  • Microphone: to speak a note. Speech is recognized on your device and never uploaded.
  • Calendars: to show your events on the Today page and check whether an event already exists. Events are added only when you choose to add them.
  • Reminders: a to-do is added to the Reminders app only when you tap to add it.
  • Location (while using the app): to show local weather, work out when to leave for an event, check work and school closures for your city, and show nearby food. Your location is not stored, and you can pick a city in Settings instead.
  • Notifications: to-do reminders, the morning summary, the evening wrap-up, and time-to-leave alerts are all scheduled on your device.

You can decline any permission or turn it off later in the iPhone Settings app. The rest of the app keeps working.

Deleting your data

Deleting a note or to-do in the app removes it from your device, and deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. If you used iCloud Sync, you can delete AI Notes data in the iPhone Settings app under [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage.

Children's Privacy

AI Notes does not knowingly collect any information from children, because it does not collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised "Last updated" date.

AI記事——隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 10 月 14 日

AI記事的設計原則是把你的資料留在裝置上。這份政策說明這個 App 會、以及不會對你的資料做什麼。

我們收集什麼資料

什麼都不收集。AI記事跟開發者本身沒有任何伺服器，不需要帳號，不會接收你的記事、待辦、照片或任何其他 App 內容，也沒有使用任何分析、廣告或追蹤 SDK。

你的資料如何被處理

  • 裝置上的 AI：找待辦與日期、辨識照片裡的文字、語音轉文字，全部在你的裝置上用 Apple Intelligence（Foundation Models 框架）、Vision 與裝置上的語音辨識完成，不會上傳。
  • 儲存：你的記事、待辦、照片與設定都存在裝置上的本機資料庫。

可選的 iCloud 同步

AI記事有一個可選的 iCloud 同步功能，預設關閉。如果你打開它，你的記事與待辦（含附加照片）會透過 Apple 的 CloudKit 存進你自己的私人 iCloud 帳號，讓同一個 Apple ID 登入的其他裝置也能看到。AI記事的開發者無法存取這份資料，也不會分享給任何第三方。

權限

  • 相機、照片：拍下或選擇海報、通知、帳單、保固卡，在裝置上找出活動與日期。
  • 麥克風：用說的記一筆。語音在裝置上辨識，不會上傳。
  • 行事曆：在「今天」顯示行程、檢查活動是否已經存在；只有你選擇加入時才會新增活動。
  • 提醒事項：只有你按下加入時，才把待辦加到「提醒事項」App。
  • 位置（使用 App 期間）：顯示當地天氣、計算幾點該出門、判斷你所在縣市的停班停課，以及附近美食。位置不會被儲存；你也可以在設定裡改成手動選縣市。
  • 通知：待辦提醒、早安、晚上收尾與該出門的提醒，都在裝置上排定。

每個權限都可以不給，或之後在 iPhone「設定」裡關掉，App 其他功能照常可用。

App 會連到的服務

以下連線都不會送出你的記事、待辦或照片內容。

服務送出的資料用途
Apple 天氣（WeatherKit）你的位置或手動選的縣市座標天氣與穿衣提醒
Apple 地圖活動地點文字、起點與終點座標、大概位置地點位置、出門時間的車程、所在縣市、附近美食
行政院人事行政總處網站一般網頁請求，不含個人資料停班停課公告

你選的新聞來源一般 RSS 請求，不含個人資料新聞頭條（設定裡打開才會使用）

這些服務依各自的隱私權政策處理請求，例如 Apple 隱私權政策。你選擇用 Google 地圖開啟地點時，會離開 AI記事，適用 Google 的隱私權政策。

刪除你的資料

在 App 裡刪除記事或待辦就會從裝置移除；刪除 App 會移除裝置上的全部資料。如果打開過 iCloud 同步，可以到 iPhone「設定」›［你的名字］› iCloud › 管理儲存空間，刪除 AI記事的資料。

兒童隱私

AI記事不會在明知情況下收集任何兒童的資料——因為它本來就不收集任何人的資料。
